Evidence matters

Real observations.
Transparent teaching scenarios.

Observed: NASA GISTEMP global annual temperature anomalies, with a fixed snapshot and stated baseline.

Synthetic: invented clinic capacity, heat, water, costs and movements. These illustrate reasoning; they are not forecasts, patient data or country risk rankings.
