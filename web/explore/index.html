Show the arithmetic

Indoor °C = outdoor + added heat + indoor offset − cooling. Heat penalty = clamp((indoor−30)×0.035, 0, 0.5). Water fraction = min(1, litres/(visits×20)). Power fraction = min(1, hours/24).

Capacity = floor(demand × (1−heat penalty) × min(water, power) × access fraction). Access fraction is 1 when open, 0.35 when closed. A coastal route closes when water ≥ road elevation, compared at the nearest 0.001 m (ties round up). All effects and costs are invented.

Flashcard: Is exposure the same as health impact?

No. Exposure describes contact with a hazard. Health impacts depend on vulnerability, protective resources and many other pathways. A toy service-capacity value cannot predict disease.
