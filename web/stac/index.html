Turn a source into a defensible map story

  1. Write the question about care access, water, energy, heat or livelihoods before choosing a layer.
  2. Record provider, product version, acquisition/issue date, geographic scale, units and license.
  3. Separate field observations, modeled estimates, projections, composite indices and fictional teaching scenarios.
  4. Check coverage, missingness and alternatives. A remotely sensed proxy cannot establish patient outcomes.
  5. Export the metadata and write what further local evidence would change your interpretation.
