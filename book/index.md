## Reading historical notebooks

These **16 legacy notebooks** preserve historical scientific statements and incomplete activities. They are not revised or verified executable end-to-end in Pyodide. Saved code outputs are cleared; code is displayed in the Book without execution. Archive notices identify original cell counts, omitted data, external services, packages and credentials needed by the old workflows. The source folder remains unchanged.

Local figures and attachments are retained with existing citations and rights. Legacy text/code, figures and resources are excluded from the repository's blanket grants for original material. Source ODbL-1.0 does not establish rights to individual figures, text or code. Remote illustrations are linked in the Book, and original DOI text remains visible; full original formatting is retained in Lab.

## Optional runnable companion labs

The **14 modern runnable lessons** are secondary exercises linked from relevant original chapters. They use compact, attributed data and explicit synthetic scenarios; their Book pages include executed results. Use the [companion orientation](notebooks/00_start_here.ipynb), [chapter-to-companion crosswalk](migration.md), or [teaching guide](teaching-guide.md) when you want to calculate or test an assumption.

The [interactive explorer](https://jltobias.github.io/JupyterLite-Climate-and-Health/explore/) adds scenario maps, a conceptual clinic scene and adaptation comparisons. The [satellite catalog](https://jltobias.github.io/JupyterLite-Climate-and-Health/stac/) supports evidence discovery. These are companion tools, not validated climate or disease predictions.

The companions distinguish NASA global observations, archived Healthsites/OpenStreetMap features and fictional clinic/service scenarios. See [data and methods](data-and-methods.md), [references](references.md) and the original chapter notices for scope. No agency endorsement, clinical guidance or accredited certification is implied.
