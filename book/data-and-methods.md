# Data, units and methods

## Separate evidence classes

| File or layer | What it represents | Units / time | Provenance and limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| `global-temperature.csv` | Observed NASA GISTEMP v4 global land-ocean annual index | °C anomaly relative to 1951–1980; complete annual J-D rows only | Raw CSV, source URL, acquisition date and SHA-256 retained in `provenance.json`; revisions after acquisition are not reflected |
| `natural-earth-land.geojson` | Natural Earth v5.1.2 1:110m land | WGS84 longitude/latitude degrees | Public domain, coarse cartography; no local flood/coastline analysis |
| `cases.json`, `study-sites.geojson` | Seven original fictional clinic scenarios at city anchors | Invented °C, elevation m, litres, power hours and visits/day | CC0; no real clinic location, service load or funding status |
| `daily-heat.csv` | Deterministic sinusoidal daily Tmax and Tmin | °C; synthetic calendar year 2025 | Not weather observations or projections; reproducible generator |
| `mobility.json` | Original movement-event teaching example | Events, route-specific persons and unique people over an invented 30 days | Definitions differ; no legal status, identifiable records or observed migration |
| `adaptations.json` | Original package assumptions | Cooling °C, road raise m, water L, power hours, arbitrary cost units | No empirical cost effectiveness or procurement claim |
| Archived Healthsites layer | Public map features from the source repository's World/World-node archive | WGS84 coordinates; archived feature inventory | © OpenStreetMap contributors / Healthsites, ODbL; incomplete and unverified current operation; never joined to fictional impacts |

Full machine-readable provenance accompanies each observational archive. Original synthetic fixtures are generated by `scripts/prepare_data.py` without randomness. Refreshing NASA requires an explicit `--refresh`; normal builds reuse the snapshot. Actual facilities, observed global temperature and invented service calculations remain separate layers.

## Scenario arithmetic

For a selected fictional clinic:

1. Indoor temperature = assumed outdoor temperature + added heat + indoor offset − package cooling.
2. Heat penalty = clamp((indoor °C − 30) × 0.035, 0, 0.5).
3. Water fraction = min(1, available litres / (daily visits × 20)). The 20 L assumption is a teaching input, not a service standard.
4. Power fraction = min(1, available hours / 24).
5. A coastal route closes at water ≥ road elevation. Both values are normalized to the nearest 0.001 m (half-millimetres rounded up) in the Python and browser models before comparison, so floating-point addition does not reverse equality. Open access multiplies capacity by 1; closure multiplies by an arbitrary 0.35 to represent an assumed alternative service fraction. Marine water does not affect inland cases.
6. Capacity = floor(daily demand × (1 − heat penalty) × min(water fraction, power fraction) × access fraction). Unmet visits = demand − capacity.

These formulas deliberately expose an invented bottleneck. They do not model patients harmed, infections, clinical outcomes, treatment adherence, hydrologic connection or actual adaptation benefits. The conceptual clinic floor and road use the same invented vertical datum. Mean sea level, MHHW, HAT, surge, waves, drainage and vertical land motion cannot be substituted for one another.

## Correct climate calculations

- Kelvin to Celsius subtracts 273.15. Cosine-latitude weighting is only an approximation for equal-longitude regular grids, not arbitrary irregular polygons.
- Ensemble 10th/50th/90th quantiles use linear interpolation at `(n−1)q`. A selected member is not the ensemble median; the same member/ensemble, year and pathway must be used in maps and associated tables. Ensemble spread is not automatically a probability distribution.
- A complete annual hot-day count requires all twelve monthly values; missing is not zero. Counts cannot reconstruct daily persistence. TXx, TNn, TXn and TNx use daily maxima/minima in the stated directions.
- DJF includes December of the preceding calendar year with the following January/February. Season labels do not assume the same hemispheric weather.
- DHW sums qualifying daily HotSpots ≥1 °C over 84 days and divides by 7. Thermal stress is not observed coral bleaching. The lesson uses synthetic data and does not reproduce an operational NOAA product.

## Responsible interpretation

Displacement events are not necessarily distinct persons or the stock currently displaced. A climate-related movement does not automatically imply refugee status. Conflict depends on contextual political, social and economic factors; the course computes no conflict score. HIV/TB continuity exercises model supplies and access, not disease causation. Any proposed real intervention needs local clinical, infrastructure, community and ecological knowledge.

## Extend with real data

Use the [satellite catalog gateway](https://jltobias.github.io/JupyterLite-Climate-and-Health/stac/) to inspect Copernicus, Digital Earth Africa and related environmental metadata. A STAC item describes a footprint, acquisition and assets, not a ready-made health metric. Check units, cloud masks, temporal alignment, pixel resolution, license and download requirements before analysis. Consult [Allen Coral Atlas](https://allencoralatlas.org/) for reef habitat context; habitat maps, thermal stress and observed bleaching are different products. No Atlas observations are fabricated or embedded into the DHW example.

Advanced CDS/NetCDF or other authenticated downloads belong in a separate local/server environment with provider terms, credential management and enough disk/RAM. Never put API keys, private patient data or precise sensitive service records in the public repository.
